Set up in two minutes

Five steps. No settings to figure out.

Install Hop on both computers, type one code, and you're done. You do this once; after that, Hop connects by itself whenever both are on.

1On your PC

Install Hop on your Windows PC

Download the installer on the PC and open it.

  1. If Windows shows a blue box saying "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Click through the installer. At the end, leave Pair a Mac now ticked.
  3. A small window shows a code. Leave it open for step 4.

Why the warning? Hop is free and not code-signed yet, so Windows doesn't recognise it. The code is public on GitHub.

3On your Mac

Let Hop use your keyboard

macOS asks whether Hop may control your computer. That's what lets your keyboard and trackpad reach the PC.

  1. Click Open System Settings when asked.
  2. Under Accessibility, switch Hop on.
  3. A small keyboard icon appears in your menu bar: that's Hop.

Hop keeps your typing private. Keystrokes go only to your own PC, encrypted, and never while a password field is open on the Mac.

Type the code from your PC

Hop finds your PC by itself and asks for the code. Type the code from the PC's screen and click Pair.

  1. Pick your PC from the list (it's usually the only one).
  2. Type the code exactly as shown. Capitals don't matter.

5That's it

Press ⌃⌥⌘→ to use your PC

Hold control, option and command, and tap the right arrow. Your pointer jumps to the PC, and everything you type goes there.

  1. Same keys with the left arrow bring you back to the Mac.
  2. A blue badge at the top of your Mac's screen shows when the PC has control.
  3. Prefer the mouse? Turn on Settings → Switch by Pushing Past the Screen Edge in Hop's menu.

Why Hop

It feels like one computer.

Hop is built for people with a Mac and a PC on the same desk, or a PC across the room on the TV.

Your Mac shortcuts, Windows results

Keep typing like you're on a Mac. Hop turns your shortcuts into what Windows expects.

⌘ C→Ctrl + C · copy
⌘ Tab→Alt + Tab · switch apps
⌥ ←→Ctrl + ← · jump a word
⌃ L→Win + L · lock the PC

See your PC from your Mac

PC across the room? Open its screen in a window on your Mac. Hop streams it live with the PC's graphics card: sharp text, smooth motion, even at 4K.

Works on the lock screen

Type your PIN, answer admin prompts, open Task Manager. Hop's small Windows service goes where most remote tools can't.

Ready for games

Trackpad taps are held long enough for games to notice, and the pointer lands on the exact pixel on every monitor.

You never lose your Mac

If the PC freezes or the Wi-Fi drops, your Mac takes its keyboard back within half a second. No key ever stays stuck.

Fast enough to forget it's there

About 5 milliseconds from Mac to PC and back on ordinary home Wi-Fi, faster on a cable. Hop itself adds a tenth of a millisecond and barely touches your CPU or battery.

Questions

Good to know.

Is Hop really free?

Yes. No trial, no ads, no account, no "pro" version. Hop is open source under the GPL-3.0, and the code is on GitHub.

Why does my Mac or PC warn me when I open it?

Apple and Microsoft charge developers to sign apps, and Hop isn't signed yet. So the first time, macOS asks you to click Open Anyway (System Settings → Privacy & Security), and Windows asks you to click More info → Run anyway.

Some antivirus apps are wary of any unsigned app that reads the keyboard. If yours removes Hop, restore it and allow it. Every release lists SHA-256 checksums, so you can check your download is the real one.

Do both computers need to be on the same Wi-Fi?

They need to be on the same home network: Wi-Fi, a cable, or one of each. Hop doesn't work over the internet, on purpose: your keystrokes never leave your home. For the lowest delay, plug the PC in with a cable.

Which computers does it work with?

Any Mac with macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, Apple silicon or Intel, and any PC with Windows 10 or 11. Hop goes one way: the Mac's keyboard and trackpad control the PC.

Does it work in games?

Yes, for most games. Hop holds every trackpad tap long enough for games to register it, and moves the pointer smoothly. A few games with strict anti-cheat ignore input from apps like Hop; for those, use a mouse plugged into the PC.

What if the PC freezes? Am I stuck?

Never. If the PC stops answering for half a second, the Mac takes its keyboard and pointer back by itself. A separate watchdog does the same even if part of Hop hangs, and the PC lets go of every key the moment the Mac goes quiet.

Can I see the PC's screen without looking at it?

Yes. Click Hop's icon in the Mac menu bar and choose Show PC's Screen. A window opens with the PC's screen, live. It's great when the PC is plugged into a TV across the room.

Something isn't working.

The troubleshooting guide covers the usual fixes: the Mac can't find the PC, Accessibility won't stick, an invisible pointer on the PC, and more. Still stuck? Open an issue.

How do I uninstall it?

Mac: Hop menu → Quit Hop, then drag Hop from Applications to the Trash. PC: Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Hop → Uninstall.
